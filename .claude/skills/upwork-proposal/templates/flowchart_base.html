Inside your Microsoft 365 and Azure tenant
1. New mail arrives
Graph pushes it to a Function in your tenant.
2. Safety rules first
Legal, HR, contracts, Purview labels, before any AI sees it.
3. Azure OpenAI
Summary, priority, draft reply. Your keys.
4. You review in Outlook
Approve, edit or reject. The mailbox is untouched until you do.
5. Only then it acts
Categorise, save to Drafts, create a task. Sending stays your click.
Mail.Send is never granted, and every step writes to your audit log
